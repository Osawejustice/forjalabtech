About Us

Forjalab Technologies is an indigenous company based in Lagos, Nigeria. We specialize in the Manufacture, Supply and Installation of Science Laboratory Furniture/Fittings, Chemicals (Reagents, etc), Lab wares (Glass, Plastic, etc.), and Equipment for all levels of Science, Technology and Education

We render services in renovation and maintenance of existing Science Laboratories and many more.

We also manufacture and install other educational furnishings such as classroom furniture, language laboratory, library furniture, computer laboratory, home economics laboratories and a whole lot of other school and office furniture.
