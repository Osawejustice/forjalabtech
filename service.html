Home · Services
One studio, the whole laboratory

From the drawing to the last beaker.

We do not drop furniture and leave. Forjalab plans the room, manufactures the benches, installs the services, stocks the apparatus, and comes back when the lab needs a second life.

Planning & design

Layouts for chemistry, biology, physics, and STEM. Wet and dry zones, teacher demonstration, storage, and the safety equipment a school actually needs.

Manufacture & installation

Benches, islands, cabinets, and fittings made for the drawing, then installed on site. Chemical-resistant tops, sinks, and the services that run through them.

Equipment procurement

ISO-certified apparatus for secondary and tertiary science — specified, supplied, and commissioned so it works on the first practical.

Glassware, plastics & chemicals

The consumables a working laboratory runs on. Glass and plastic lab wares, plus reagents stored and labelled for school use.

Renovation & maintenance

Existing labs brought up to a standard students can work in. We keep what is sound, replace what is not, and do it without emptying the building.

Campus & clinic

Libraries, ICT, home economics, cafeterias, classroom furniture, and hospital equipment from the same workshop that builds the labs.

The sequence

A process schools can brief against.

01

Discover

Site visit or photographs, subject list, class size, and budget. We will tell you what the room can honestly hold.

02

Design

A layout with services, storage, and safety marked. You approve the drawing before anything is cut.

03

Build

Manufacture in Lagos. Installation by the people who built the furniture — not a third crew who has never seen the drawing.

04

Equip

Apparatus, glassware, reagents, and a handover that includes how to look after the room.

Free consultation. Practical advice.

No obligation, no generic catalogue dump. Tell us the room and we will tell you what it needs.
