How we work

From empty room to working laboratory.

Schools do not need a catalogue. They need a partner who will walk the brief, the budget, and the building — then manufacture what the room actually requires.

01 — Discover

Walk the brief

Curriculum, class size, services, and the money you actually have. We listen before we draw.

02 — Design

Plan the work

Layout, wet and dry zones, storage, ventilation, and teacher demonstration — so the room is safe to use on day one.

03 — Build

Make and install

Benches, islands, cabinets, and fittings manufactured for the drawing, then installed on site in Lagos and beyond.

04 — Equip

Stock the science

Glassware, reagents, apparatus, and aftercare — so the lab is not an empty piece of furniture.
