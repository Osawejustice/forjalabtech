The studio

Built in Lagos, for Nigerian science.

Forjalab Technologies is an indigenous company with offices in Lagos and Kano. We specialise in the manufacture, supply, and installation of science laboratory furniture, fittings, chemicals, lab wares, and equipment — for every level of science education.

What we believe

A laboratory is a place of discovery, or it is just furniture.

We design so students can work without the room getting in the way. Chemical-resistant tops. Layouts a teacher can supervise. Storage that keeps reagents safe. Then we make the budget honest — practical advice before a single board is cut.

Beyond science labs we furnish the rest of the campus: classrooms, language laboratories, libraries, ICT, home economics, and offices.

How we hold ourselves

Three things we will not compromise.

Safety first

The room has to be safe on a busy Tuesday, not only in the brochure. Wet services, storage, and sightlines are designed together — the way a science teacher would ask for them.

Made to be used

Granite and chemical-resistant tops, frames that take weight, fittings that survive students. Furniture is the foundation of curiosity. It should not fail in the first term.

Budget, spoken plainly

We listen to the brief and tell you what the money can actually do. Free consultation. No catalogue theatre. A plan you can take to a proprietor or a PTA.
